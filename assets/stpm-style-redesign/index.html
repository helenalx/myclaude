Style reference review — endurmenntunarferdir.is redesign

You asked for the new site to feel like Smart Teachers Play More. Below is a read on STPM's visual language, a quick verdict on each of the sample links against that direction, and the concrete ideas worth carrying into the WordPress redesign — no page mockup yet, just the thinking to sign off on first.

Prepared by Helen · based on the STPM homepage and the sample links shared

Recommendation

STPM as the single style reference

Smart Teachers Play More is the clearest, most consistent style to build from: a soft mint/teal palette against white, cream and pale-green section bands, fully rounded pill buttons, photos cropped into circles, and one confident yellow call-to-action color. Of the sample links, none matches that warmth closely enough to sit alongside it as a second reference — they'd pull the design in a more corporate or generic direction. Best approach: keep STPM as the only visual reference, and treat the samples below purely for isolated ideas (a content block, a page rhythm), not for look and feel.

Ideas for the redesign

What carries over from STPM, applied to your content

These are the concrete moves — not a built page yet, just what we'd design toward once you sign off on the direction.

1

Mint/teal palette + one gold accent

Replace the current bright cyan/orange with a softer teal-on-white system and a single warm gold for buttons — reads calmer and closer to STPM.

2

Pill buttons + circular photo crops

STPM's two clearest signatures. Round every button fully, and crop course/destination photos into circles instead of square cards.

3

Destination grid in the hero

STPM opens with a 2×2 country grid + a pill button under each. Your four destinations — Amsterdam, London, Salzburg, Berlin — map onto that pattern directly.

4

Alternating pastel section bands

Instead of a flat white page, alternate white / pale-mint / cream backgrounds between sections, the way STPM separates mission, courses, and testimonials.

5

Funding band, STPM-book-banner style

Your Vonarsjóður/Erasmus+ funding info is currently plain text — worth turning into its own bold teal banner, the way STPM promotes its book.

6

Newsletter signup before the footer

New addition, borrowed from STPM's community sign-up band — a simple name/email capture to keep past participants warm for next year's trips.
